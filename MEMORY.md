## Domain Models

### Technology Readiness Framework
Cipher uses a custom TRL-inspired scale:
- **TRL 1-3** (Concept/PoC): Interesting physics, no path to production
- **TRL 4-5** (Lab validated): Works in controlled conditions, key parameters measured
- **TRL 6-7** (Pilot/Prefoundry): Demonstrated at scale, foundry engagement started
- **TRL 8-9** (Production/Deployed): Shipping or committed roadmap

### Source Quality Tiers
- **Tier 1:** Peer-reviewed, independent verification, raw data available
- **Tier 2:** Industry conference, reputable lab, plausible methodology
- **Tier 3:** Company whitepaper, preprint, simulation-only
- **Tier 4:** Press release, blog post, unverified claim
