🔑 Key Takeaways

Analog CIM Is Not Ready for LLMs High Confidence

The ADC/DAC conversion overhead consumes 60–90% of total system energy and 75–90% of die area. No analog CIM accelerator has been demonstrated end-to-end on a production-scale LLM (70B+) with competitive throughput-per-watt vs. NVIDIA H100/B200.

No Production Analog KV Cache Exists High Confidence

Closest published architecture (Jülich/RWTH, Sep 2024) achieves 10⁴× latency reduction for GPT-2-scale models but requires custom initialization and sliding-window attention — not a general KV cache solution.

FeFET Leads Analog Memory Race Medium Confidence

TSMC, GlobalFoundries, and SK hynix have FeFET roadmaps at 28–16 nm. Still 2–3 years from LLM-relevant deployment due to wake-up effects, read-disturb, and interlayer defects.

Bayesian Optimization = Whitespace High Confidence

Foundries deploy BO for process optimization (lithography, etch) but zero published work applies BO to FeFET write-pulse tuning, ReRAM conductance programming, or gain-cell drift compensation. This is a genuinely uncontested capability.

STT-MRAM Is the Only Volume Shipper High Confidence

Among memristor-class technologies, only STT-MRAM is in production (Samsung 28nm, TSMC 22nm, GF 22FDX). ReRAM/PCM analog CIM remains lab-scale with a 3–4 order-of-magnitude gap to useful networks.

Market Reality Check Medium Confidence

Memristor market forecasts range $0.6–2.6B by 2030, but >90% of that is MRAM and PCM storage. True analog CIM memristor revenue likely <$100M by 2030. The technology is real; the commercial path is narrow.
