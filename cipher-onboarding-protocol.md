## Practice Assignments

### Assignment A: Terrain Map — "The Competitive Landscape of AI Property Management Tools"
**Scope:** Map 5–7 tools (Buildium, AppFolio, RentRedi, DoorLoop, etc.) against Laere's specific needs (Busti property, NY state compliance, budget constraints).
**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Each tool evaluated against ≥5 specific Laere criteria (not generic feature lists)
- Pricing data current and sourced
- API capabilities explicitly noted (yes/no/unknown)
- NY-specific compliance notes included
- Risk matrix: what could make this map wrong in 6 months?
**Target Length:** 2,000–3,000 words
**Time Budget:** 6–8 hours
**Reviewer:** Grace

### Assignment B: Technology Readiness Assessment — "FeFET Memory for Edge AI Inference"
**Scope:** Assess FeFET (ferroelectric FET) technology readiness for low-power edge AI inference. Focus on production timeline, foundry availability, and comparison to existing solutions (Flash, SRAM, ReRAM).
**Evaluation Criteria:**
- TRL score assigned with explicit justification
- ≥3 foundry roadmaps referenced (TSMC, GF, Samsung)
- Cost estimates included where available
- Invalidation criteria: "This assessment would be wrong if..."
- Specific recommendation: should Laere monitor, invest research time, or ignore?
**Target Length:** 5,000–8,000 words
**Time Budget:** 10–12 hours
**Reviewer:** Grace + Shawn (for strategic recommendation)

### Assignment C: Intelligence Brief — "Signal Scan: What Changed in Analog Computing This Week"
**Scope:** Weekly brief format. Review all sources Cipher monitors for analog computing. Surface 2–3 significant changes with context and implications.
**Evaluation Criteria:**
- 500–1,000 words
- Each signal includes: what changed, why it matters, confidence level, source
- At least one signal must be negative or contrarian (not just "progress happened")
- Clear action implication for Laere or Shawn
- Produced within 4 hours of starting
**Reviewer:** Grace (format) + Shawn (signal quality)

## Graduation Criteria

Cipher is considered **fully autonomous** when ALL of the following are met:

### Output Quality (Non-negotiable)
- [ ] 3 consecutive deliverables pass Grace's quality review with ≤2 minor revisions each
- [ ] Executive summaries consistently decision-oriented (Grace confirms)
- [ ] Source quality: ≥60% Tier 1–2 across all deliverables in Month 1
- [ ] No high-confidence claims without supporting evidence (Grace audit)

### Operational Discipline (Non-negotiable)
- [ ] Weekly intelligence brief delivered on schedule for 4 consecutive weeks
- [ ] All deliverables committed to git within 1 hour of completion
- [ ] MEMORY.md updated within 48 hours of every major research completion
- [ ] Watch list operational with ≥3 monitored domains

### Cross-Agent Value (Non-negotiable)
- [ ] At least 1 intelligence snippet delivered to Grace that shaped a decision
- [ ] At least 1 mock Orthia handoff completed successfully
- [ ] Proactive identification: at least 1 unsolicited research topic justified and approved

### Autonomy Demonstration (Non-negotiable)
- [ ] Self-assigned terrain map produced without prompting, meeting quality bar
- [ ] Methodology reflection written and incorporated into workflow
- [ ] Can articulate his 3 hard boundaries, 3 autonomous decisions, and 3 escalation triggers

### Shawn Sign-Off (Final Gate)
- [ ] Shawn reviews Month 1 research portfolio and confirms Cipher is "useful, not just interesting"
- [ ] Shawn approves authority tier assignments as documented in role definition
- [ ] Shawn green-lights the 60–90 day autonomy expansion plan

**Graduation Date Target:** June 6, 2026 (Day 30 + 1 week buffer)
**If criteria not met:** 2-week extension with specific remediation plan from Grace.
