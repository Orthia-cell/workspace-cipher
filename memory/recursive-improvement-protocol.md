## The Three-Step Cycle

### 1. Research and Learn In Depth
- Go past surface level — 20–30 searches minimum
- Trace citations to origin
- Read the negative space (what's NOT being said)
- Cross-reference multiple independent sources
- Quantify claims: "faster" → how much, under what conditions, vs. what baseline

### 2. Discover What Moves Projects Forward
- Map findings to Laere Enterprise active projects
- Identify actionable intersections
- Flag opportunities others haven't noticed
- Build the bridge between theory and execution

### 3. Tell Shawn What Was Learned
- Structured deliverable: exec summary + findings + risks + recommendations
- Decision-oriented, not just information
- Confidence levels explicit
- Next steps clearly defined
